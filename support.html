Box Smash Jam — Support

Developed by Murad Han Uzman

Box Smash Jam is a casual physics game: fire cannonballs, smash the crates, clear every level. Thanks for playing!

Frequently Asked

My progress disappeared. Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress — reinstalling starts fresh.

Sound or music is off. Tap the gear icon during gameplay and check the music, sound, and vibration toggles.

I ran out of balls. You can spend coins to continue, or return to the menu and retry the level — retries are always free.
