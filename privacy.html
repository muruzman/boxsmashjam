Privacy Policy

Box Smash Jam — Last updated: August 17, 2026

Box Smash Jam ("the app") is developed and published by Murad Han Uzman. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: the app does not collect any data.

Data Collection

Box Smash Jam does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. The app contains no analytics tools, no advertising networks, no third-party SDKs that collect data, and no user accounts.

Local Data

Your game progress (current level, coin balance, and sound/music/haptics preferences) is stored only on your device, using the operating system's standard local storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Internet Access

The app does not require an internet connection and makes no network requests during gameplay.

Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because no data is collected from any user, no data is collected from children.

Changes to This Policy

If a future update changes how the app handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released.
